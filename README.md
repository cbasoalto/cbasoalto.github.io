# Cristian Basoalto - Personal Academic Website

This is my personal academic website showcasing my research projects, technical skills, and professional experience.

## About Me

I am a researcher focused on robotics, AI, and advanced control systems. As a professor at the University of Bío-Bío, I teach courses in robotics and control while leading projects in renewable energy and autonomous systems. I hold a Master's in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Concepción, where I specialized in Model Predictive Control.

## Website Features

- **Research Projects**: Showcase of my main research work including:
  - Model Predictive Control Applied to an Autonomous Indoor Blimp
  - Convolutional Neural Network for Robotic Apple Harvesting
  - LQR Control Applied to an Inverted Pendulum

- **Technical Skills**: Comprehensive list of my expertise in:
  - Control Systems (MPC, LQR, Advanced Control Theory)
  - Programming Languages (Python, C++, MATLAB/Simulink)
  - Robotics & AI (ROS2, Computer Vision, Neural Networks)
  - Tools & Platforms (IsaacSim, Gazebo, OpenCV, TensorFlow)

- **Professional Links**: Direct access to my email, GitHub, and Google Scholar profiles
